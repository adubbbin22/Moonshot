# Round 2: Growth marketer

## Endorse / challenge / merge

- **Endorse:** the resolution spec plus horizon guard is the prerequisite for everything. Ship it first.
- **Merge:** Call → Reveal with the slider. Tap a side first, then optionally refine to a percentage. A forced slider costs activation.
- **Endorse:** executable truth. Paper fills at mid flatter users, real money then disappoints, and that kills paper-to-real conversion.
- **Endorse the firewall,** but cap "every match" at 3–5 options so the screen doesn't turn into a menu.
- **Challenge the season letter:** the shared version must drop the return multiple. Calibration and best call only.
- **Challenge, then endorse, the "Crowd vs. Wall Street" board,** at the event level only. Tied to tickers, it looks like a research report.
- **Challenge the Barbell Account as the first swing.** Sequence it after about 50k forecasters.

## Positions

- **T1:** the free forecasting game is the acquisition channel. The paid options sleeve ($10/mo) is the business, and the only way to keep CAC under $60.
  - *Free:* calls, the reveal, the public record, cards, private leagues.
  - *Paid:* real-money bets with autopilot, Thesis Pulse monitoring of your positions, unlimited Thesis Tree (3 a month free), deep season-letter calibration.
  - *The conversion trigger, proven disagreement:* a user beats the reference on 5+ resolved calls in a theme, and their new forecast diverges by 15+ points. Then show "Want to act on it? Three shots a month."
- **T2:** the artifact is the chain ("Hormuz stays disrupted 60% × Brent >$100 50% × tankers rally 55% = 16.5%"), stopping at the mechanism link with no ticker. Not a "thesis map" (homework), and not "bear case survived" (the LLM grading itself).
- **T3:** the resolution card marks each link: Event ✓ · Mechanism ✓/✗ · Trade ✓/✗. "Called the event. Missed the trade." Cards never imply money either way.
- **T4:** never put a number on a card that prices a different claim than the user made.
  - *World questions:* a licensed prediction-market price, or else the blind-crowd median (n ≥ 50).
  - *Price questions:* spread ÷ width, scored against the tail-adjusted baseline.
  - *v1 public cards:* question-level only, until counsel approves a ticket template that satisfies Rule 2220.
- **T5:** one surface, **Thesis Pulse**: holding / weakening / broken status, data-resolved monthly checkpoints as the signposts, and one Sunday Thesis Check.
  - Default cap of 2 pushes a week, hard cap 3.
  - Breaking questions are opt-in by theme and count against the cap.
  - Protective alerts are exempt.
  - No price pushes, nothing about securities the user doesn't hold, no buy call-to-action (deep links go to the thesis).
  - Quiet hours 9pm–8am.
- **T6:** it doesn't break the story if the story changes. A $100 shot is dinner money for 25–40-year-olds with an index fund. Sell "three shots a month", not "small bets"; $0 forecasting is the small entry. Real-money minimum $300/mo, and drop the $100 tier.
- **T7:** rename to **Barbell**, pending trademark and App Store checks.
  - "moonshots" stays as the noun for bets
  - the night sky is how you see them
  - Fund I is the season
  - the Barbell Account grows into the name

  One-liner: *"Make the call. See the market's odds. Bet small only when you disagree."* Keep "beat Wall Street" out of paid ads; use "vs. the market".
- **T8:** a merge, in order:
  1. "Beat the market's odds" is the spine.
  2. Fund I night sky is its emotional skin; it solves the churn after the first loss.
  3. The Barbell Account is the 2028 economics.
- **T9:**
  - The Fund is a 12-month vintage; league seasons last a quarter.
  - Private leagues of 5–20. Public divisions only after 20 resolved forecasts.
  - Weekly streaks only, with one freeze.
  - Five badges total.
  - "Honest exit" for marking your own thesis broken, not for closing a position.
  - The season letter: the LLM writes the prose, code supplies every number.

## Final top 5

1. **Resolvable questions with Call → Reveal.** Metric: 3+ calls in the first session, 40%.
2. **Called It cards** (question-level, timestamped, no dollars). Metric: organic K-factor from cards of at least 0.3.
3. **Thesis Pulse.** Metric: weekly check completion among users with an open thesis, 50%.
4. **The paid sleeve:** one-glance bet screen on executable prices, firewalled tree, autopilot. Metric: free-to-paid conversion within 90 days of eligibility, 8–10%.
5. **Fund I seasons:** night sky, private leagues, season letter. Metric: share of users still active 30 days after their first bet expires worthless.

## What changed

- **Called It cards need resolution first.** The resolution spec is the cheapest and most important build.
- **The card had the wrong number:** FRO odds ≠ Hormuz odds.
- **Retention means surviving the first loss.** The free tier is non-negotiable.
